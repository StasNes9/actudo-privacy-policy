Actudo Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 14, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Actudo ("we", "our", or "the app") handles information when you use the Actudo productivity and anti-procrastination application.

1. Information Stored by the App

Actudo may store app progress and preferences, such as completed actions, coins, experience points, achievements, streaks, selected characters, settings, and notification preferences. This information may be stored locally on your device.

2. Analytics

Actudo uses Google Firebase Analytics to understand app usage and improve the app. Depending on the app configuration and your device settings, analytics services may collect information such as app interactions, app instance identifiers, device and app information, and diagnostic or usage events.

For more information about how Google processes data, please review Google's Privacy Policy .

3. Advertising and Rewards

Actudo may display advertisements, including rewarded advertisements that offer in-app rewards after an eligible ad is completed. Advertising providers may process device identifiers, ad interaction information, and other data as described in their own privacy policies and according to the app's configuration and applicable consent requirements.

Information about Google's advertising practices is available in Google's Advertising Policies and Technologies information .

4. Notifications

Actudo may schedule local notifications on your device, for example to remind you about available actions or app activity. You can manage notification permissions in your device settings.

5. How Information Is Used

Information may be used to provide app features, save progress, deliver notifications, understand app usage, maintain app performance, and support advertising where enabled.

6. Sharing of Information

Actudo does not sell your personal information. Data may be processed by service providers, such as analytics or advertising providers, when those services are enabled. These providers process information under their own terms and privacy policies.

7. Data Retention and Deletion

App progress stored locally may remain on your device until it is deleted by clearing the app's data or uninstalling the app. Information handled by third-party services is retained according to their applicable policies and settings.

8. Children's Privacy

Actudo is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children in violation of applicable law. If you believe that information has been collected improperly, please contact us.

9. Security

We take reasonable steps to protect information, but no method of electronic storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the app or its data practices change. The updated version will be published on this page with a revised date.

11. Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or Actudo's data practices, contact us at actudoapp@gmail.com.
